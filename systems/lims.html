Sample traceability

MCDC LIMS

Laboratory information management links subjects, collections, sample derivatives, storage and processing history.

← All systems

What the system supports

  • Register samples and track tubes, aliquots and parent–child relationships.
  • Associate specimens with collection metadata and laboratory workflows.
  • Navigate storage locations and sample history.
  • Browse read-only LIMS snapshots through the Sample Tracker and WebNGS workflows where configured.

A typical workflow

  1. Register received material with the appropriate project and identifiers.
  2. Record processing steps and the derivatives they produce.
  3. Maintain sample locations and retain the history needed for downstream review.

Outputs

Sample records, lineage, storage information and laboratory workflow history.

Scope and availability

The legacy LIMS, read-only snapshot viewers and the LIMS2 prototype have different roles. The service-per-module LIMS2 repository describes a prototype; it is not evidence that those modules are deployed.

Access, configuration and validation requirements are agreed with the project and laboratory teams for the intended use.
