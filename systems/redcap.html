Clinical data capture

REDCap & study data capture

Clinical cohorts can use REDCap, dedicated portals or agreed project forms to provide the metadata needed by laboratory workflows.

← All systems

What the system supports

  • Agree the clinical variables, visits and identifiers required for the study.
  • Link laboratory samples to pseudonymised subject identifiers.
  • Define controlled data imports and exports with the study team.
  • Use minimal metadata where a full clinical data collection portal is unnecessary.

A typical workflow

  1. Agree the study dataset and approvals.
  2. Capture the required variables in the selected study system.
  3. Transfer agreed identifiers and metadata to the laboratory workflow.

Outputs

Study-specific metadata and agreed exports for sample linkage.

Scope and availability

REDCap is an option described in the platform overview. The reviewed repositories do not establish a deployed REDCap instance or a live connection to the LIMS.

Access, configuration and validation requirements are agreed with the project and laboratory teams for the intended use.
