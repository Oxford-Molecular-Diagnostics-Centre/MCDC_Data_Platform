Sample logistics

Sample Tracker

Sample transfer workflows connect incoming manifests, barcode receipt, storage, usage and laboratory handovers.

← All systems

What the system supports

  • Upload sample transfer manifests from Excel.
  • Receive tubes by barcode and record receipt conditions.
  • Assign storage positions and record usage status.
  • Generate handover manifests in Excel or PDF.
  • Browse project, sample, storage and lineage information from a read-only LIMS snapshot.

A typical workflow

  1. Select the project and upload the transfer manifest.
  2. Receive the expected tubes and assign laboratory storage.
  3. Record use and generate documentation for onward transfer.

Outputs

Sample receipt records, storage and usage history, transfer documents and read-only LIMS views.

Scope and availability

The standalone Tracker portal and WebNGS tracking module have separate persistence and migration arrangements. LIMS snapshot views are read-only and reflect the available snapshot rather than a live database connection.

Access, configuration and validation requirements are agreed with the project and laboratory teams for the intended use.
