EQA Scheme Management

WebEQA tracks external quality assessment schemes, distributions, responsibilities and reporting activity.

← All systems

What the system supports

  • Maintain scheme details, yearly records and distribution metadata.
  • Assign workflow responsibilities and due dates.
  • Capture report receipt, outcomes, comments and follow-up actions.
  • Review audit history, configuration snapshots and spreadsheet exports.
  • Send configured reminders for outstanding workflow stages.

A typical workflow

  1. Configure a scheme, its yearly information and workflow.
  2. Register a distribution and progress its assigned tasks.
  3. Record results and follow-up, then review the workflow report and audit history.

Outputs

Scheme and distribution records, workflow reports, Excel exports and audit history.

Scope and availability

The application is designed for a single site. Access depends on user roles, and notifications require configuration. EQA record keeping supports laboratory oversight; it does not itself confer accreditation.

Access, configuration and validation requirements are agreed with the project and laboratory teams for the intended use.
